Bingo is a fictional character from a cartoon show called "Bluey". Bingo is the younges member of the family. There are 4 members in the family: Bingo, Bluey(Bingo sister), Bandit(Dad), and Chilli(Mom). Bingo is a 4 year old red heeler pup who loves to play imaginative games with her sister Bluey. Bingo is known for being sweet, caring, and sometimes a bit shy. She often looks up to her older sister and enjoys spending time with her family.
